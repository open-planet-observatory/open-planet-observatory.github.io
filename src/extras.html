Live cams

Watch the planet, not the people

Official cameras pointed at volcanoes, wildlife, the sky and Earth from orbit. Each card opens the owner's own page; nothing is re-streamed or recorded here. Some cams are seasonal or only light up in daylight.

Stories from NASA

Official NASA channels, each paired with the layer it explains. Specific videos can be added as cards here; NASA's own videos are public domain, embedded or linked under YouTube's rules (never behind a paywall, never with ads on the player).

A cam is listed only if

  • It's run by or with the owner of the place: a science agency, park, observatory, aquarium or nonprofit
  • It points at nature, the sky, space or a science site
  • People in view are too small or far away to be identified
  • We link to it rather than copy, store or re-stream the video

Never listed

  • Street, traffic, city-square and business cams
  • Private or unsecured cameras found by scanning the internet
  • Beach, campus or venue cams where faces or plates are readable
  • Any cam in or near an active conflict zone

If something changes

  • A cam whose view changes to show identifiable people is removed
  • Owners can ask for removal at any time, no questions asked
  • Spotted a problem? Report it on the project's issue tracker
